Two WebRTC peers sometimes cannot open a connection to one another, and something on the public Internet has to carry the traffic. turnd is that something: it answers STUN, relays TURN, mints short-lived credentials only after an OpenID Connect login through go-authn, and runs a rendezvous, so a host opens a room, shares one link, and the guest's answer comes back on its own.

It is the relay the go-tex playground uses for live collaboration over go-crdt/collab.

A leaked room link lets a stranger knock, not enter

A guest who joins a room is pending: it can only receive. It cannot send, cannot get credentials, and counts against a separate pending cap and a pending TTL. The host sees the knock and decides; only an admitted guest produces a join and is charged to the host's room. The relay itself refuses to carry traffic to loopback, private, link-local or its own addresses, except between two clients' relayed ports, so the host's SSH, HTTPS and TURN listeners stay unreachable through it.
